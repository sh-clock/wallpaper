This live wallpaper is an engine-faithful, software-rendered recreation of the Silent Hill 1 fog street: the scene rendering, the fog, the snow and rain particles and the environment presets are reimplemented 1:1 from the original PlayStation game logic and its software rasterizer.

The app is a non-commercial fan project. No donations, no ads, no telemetry, no tracking — the wallpaper runs completely offline.

### What is rendered?

- The Silent Hill 1 town street scene, rasterized in software exactly like the PSX original (320x224 internal resolution with PSX wobble and 15-bit color)
- The game's own environment system: all 21 MAP_EFFECTS_INFOS presets with their fog distances, fog colors, world tints and flashlight lighting
- The game's own particle system: snow and rain with wind, including the engine's smooth weather cross-fades
- Auto Mode: the wallpaper follows your device clock and switches between the day street (06:00-18:00) and the night street (18:00-06:00)
