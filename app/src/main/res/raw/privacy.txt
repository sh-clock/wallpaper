Fog Wallpaper is a fully offline live wallpaper. It does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

Network access: none. The app contains no ads, no analytics, no telemetry and no tracking — it never opens a network connection.

Permissions: the app requests no dangerous permissions. It uses the standard live-wallpaper system integration (SET_WALLPAPER), optional haptic feedback (VIBRATE) and file access only through the system file picker when you explicitly import a camera config or scene assets.

Storage: settings, camera configs and imported scene assets remain in the app's private storage on your device. Nothing is shared with other apps or with the developer.

The wallpaper engine runs entirely on your device's CPU or GPU and renders continuously while visible. No data leaves the device.
