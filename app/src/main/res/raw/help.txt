#### The wallpaper stays black after applying it?

Android runs the wallpaper engine together with the system preview. If the preview wins the race, the real wallpaper may need a moment to take over the surface. Turning the screen off and back on, or re-applying the wallpaper, fixes this immediately.

#### Changed environment settings are not visible?

Settings are pushed to the running engine the moment you change them. If the wallpaper service was stopped by the system (some launchers aggressively kill background services), re-apply the wallpaper once.

#### What do the environment presets mean?

They are the game's own environment configurations (MAP_EFFECTS_INFOS), each with its own fog distance, fog color, world tint and lighting. The names and key parameters are shown in the selection list exactly as the engine carries them.

#### What does Auto Mode do?

During the day (06:00-18:00 by your device clock) the wallpaper uses the day street preset; at night (18:00-06:00) it uses the night street preset. It re-checks every minute and follows time zone and manual clock changes.

#### Battery usage?

The renderer is a pure software rasterizer capped at 30 fps and idles with nearly zero CPU whenever the wallpaper is not visible (screen off, another app in front). No wakelocks, no network.
