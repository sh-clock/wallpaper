GeoNames World Cities Database
=================================

The built-in world city database of Sun-Time Auto Mode is derived from
the GeoNames "cities15000" dataset (https://www.geonames.org).

(c) GeoNames, licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
License (CC BY 4.0). The full license text is available at
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The app ships a filtered subset: every national capital and every city
of one million or more inhabitants (city names, country names and
decimal-degree WGS84 coordinates).

Attribution: (c) GeoNames, CC BY 4.0, https://www.geonames.org
